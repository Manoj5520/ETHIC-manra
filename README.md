# ETHIC × MANRA — Strategic Reference

This is the standalone, public reference site for ETHIC's evolution toward **Behavior Engineering**, and MANRA's role as the strategic-intelligence, discovery and qualification system behind the company.

## The relationship in one sentence

**ETHIC is the company; MANRA is the system that helps ETHIC decide which human problem has earned the right to become a product.**

ETHIC owns the product commitment and outcome. MANRA provides the research, analysis, evidence and recommendation. They are connected, but they are not interchangeable brands.

## Brand meaning

- **ETHIC — Behavior Engineering Company:** the intended company category. The current commercial vehicle being explored is affordable, portable, everyday physical products; this is the initial route to market, not the full definition of the company.
- **Fingerprint:** a visual metaphor for recognisable patterns in human behaviour that are shaped by person, task and context. It is not a claim about biometric identification or surveillance technology.
- **“Identity Built Into Everything”:** intended behaviour informs the problem selected, physical form, affordances, interaction, usability and maintenance from the start. The tagline is not a promise to place a logo on every object or a styling layer applied after engineering.
- **MANRA:** the internal strategic-intelligence and discovery system behind ETHIC—not a separate product catalogue or external consulting business. This reference does not invent an acronym expansion for either brand name.

## Applied case study — Polygons Design

The Polygons fold-flat measuring-spoon case study follows the MANRA method and precedes ETHIC's own F1 worked example. It illustrates an external product-design precedent: two fold-flat tools cover six measuring sizes and compact for storage. It is a learning reference—not an ETHIC product, an F1 candidate, or proof that ETHIC's thesis is validated.

The Shark Tank clip supplied for review was analysed for the design move and its claims. The public page uses a publisher-linked product video rather than redistributing the television segment. Business metrics are labelled as reported claims, not audited financial statements, proof of profit, or ETHIC results.

## Evidence discipline

- Observation is not validation.
- A workaround signals behaviour to investigate; it does not prove demand.
- A candidate is not a cleared P1.
- A cleared P1 is not automatically a validated product.
- Validation is not the same as scalable unit economics.
- Do not describe an IP position, partnership, prototype, sale or KPI as achieved unless supporting evidence exists.
