# ETHIC × MANRA — Behavior Engineering Reference

This repository contains the separate ETHIC strategic reference website. It is intentionally separate from the personal portfolio repository.

## Core relationship

**ETHIC is the company. MANRA is the strategic-intelligence, discovery and qualification system behind it.**

The company’s intended category is Behavior Engineering. Affordable everyday physical products are the current first-product search, not a permanent limit on the company definition. This page follows the shift from gadget-first ideation to problem- and behaviour-first discovery.

## What is on the page

1. ETHIC identity, company relationship with MANRA, fingerprint meaning and “Identity Built Into Everything.”
2. The change from early gadget ideas to evidence-led discovery.
3. Tooth powder → toothpaste format evolution, with historical nuance. The page does not claim that one single friction caused the change or that powders disappeared globally.
4. F1–F4 micro-frictions with a cable-organiser example and the Caulking Finger.
5. F5 human-friction + technology, using NEO 1 as an illustrative reference—not a product assumed to be defective.
6. F6–F11 technological / workflow frictions, anchored in Dyson V8 cordless vacuum and Dyson CameraJet reference examples.
7. The user-supplied fold-flat spoon video as an applied external design case. The player uses a native HTML5 video tag and no YouTube embed.
8. The historical C1–C5 candidate audit, kept distinct from the F1–F11 taxonomy.
9. The separate F5 / F11 / Pattern 01 + Pattern 02 meanings.
10. Intended differentiation thesis, founder section, target KPIs and current stop point.

## Numbering rule

- **F1–F4:** micro-frictions.
- **F5:** human micro-friction intersecting with technology at a bounded workflow/system level.
- **F6–F11:** technology-led / macro frictions; **F11** specifically covers open-technology interoperability and handoff.
- **C1–C5:** the five earlier physical candidate ideas. They are not F1–F5 track labels.
- **Pattern 01 + Pattern 02:** a separate product-design hypothesis—commercial simplicity plus meaningful capability transformation.
- **P1/P2:** internal qualification gates, not industry certifications.

## Current status — not a launch announcement

- P2: unclaimed.
- Product / paid validation, unit economics, patentability and commercial scale are not established.
