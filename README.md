# ETHIC × MANRA — Behavior Engineering

**ETHIC is the company. MANRA is the strategic-intelligence and discovery system behind it.**

This repository hosts the separate ETHIC × MANRA website. It is intentionally independent of the personal portfolio repository.

## The thesis

ETHIC is being developed around **Behavior Engineering**: begin with the task a person is trying to complete, find the resistance they repeatedly face, study the workarounds and existing products, then decide whether a better intervention is justified.

**“Identity Built Into Everything”** means the intended human outcome should shape the object, interaction, material, usability and upkeep from the start. The fingerprint is a metaphor for behavioural patterns and individual context—not a biometric-technology claim.

MANRA is the intelligence layer: observe, research, compare, qualify and recommend. Evidence decides whether an idea should proceed, narrow or stop.

## Numbering and evidence rules

- **F1–F4:** micro-friction types.
- **F5:** human friction intersecting with technology in a bounded task/workflow.
- **F6–F11:** technology-led and wider workflow friction types; F11 specifically examines interoperability and handoffs.
- **C1–C5:** earlier physical product-candidate ideas. These are not synonyms for F1–F5.
- **Pattern 01 + Pattern 02:** a separate product-design hypothesis, not a qualified candidate.
- **P1/P2:** internal qualification gates, not industry certifications.

The site separates evidence labels:
- **Observed:** seen or documented in a specific context.
- **Hypothesis:** plausible, but not yet established.
- **Verified:** the particular claim has passed a suitable check.

Pictures, product launches and manufacturer claims do not prove novelty, unmet demand, patentability, clinical superiority or product-market fit.
